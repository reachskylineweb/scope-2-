About the Event

Empowering clinical mastery through live surgical innovation, AI diagnostics, and global collaboration.

OCTOBER 14-16, 2026 • PREMIER HOSPITAL

Pioneering Modern Gastrointestinal Endoscopy

The National Endoscopy Conference 2026 represents the flagship endoscopy summit for interventional gastroenterology in South Asia. Uniting 50+ international visiting professors and clinical directors, the 3-day event features unedited 4K live procedure transmissions, computer-assisted AI mucosal screening, and hands-on tactile simulation laboratories.
