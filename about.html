WHY SCOPE 2027?

BECAUSE WATCHING IS NOT ENOUGH.

At SCOPE 2027, every session is designed to answer the questions that matter in real-world endoscopy. From basic principles to advanced interventions, SCOPE 2027 brings together concepts, techniques and clinical decision-making.

Q2 When should I do it?
Q3 How should I do it?
Q4 What if it fails?
Q5 When should I stop?

LIVE ENDOSCOPY

The procedure. The decision. The discussion.

Watch advanced therapeutic procedures performed live by experts. The emphasis is not merely on demonstrating the procedure, but on understanding key clinical decision points:

  • ✓ Patient Selection
  • ✓ Pre-procedure Planning
  • ✓ Technical Strategy
  • ✓ Device Selection
  • ✓ Troubleshooting & Complication Management
  • ✓ Post-procedure Care

HANDS-ON WORKSHOPS

From watching to doing.

Our hands-on workshops are designed to translate knowledge into procedural skills. Dedicated training opportunities cover selected areas of:

• ERCP
• EUS
• Colonoscopy
• Upper GI Endoscopy
• Third-space Endoscopy
• Endoscopic Resection
• Endoscopic Suturing
• Manometry
