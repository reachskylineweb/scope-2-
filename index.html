MESSAGE FROM THE CHAIRMAN

Taking the Next Step in Therapeutic Endoscopy

Dear Colleagues and Friends,

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to SCOPE 2027, an international gathering dedicated to the rapidly evolving world of therapeutic endoscopy.

The field of endoscopy has moved far beyond diagnosis. Today, endoscopists are performing increasingly complex interventions that were once considered the domain of surgery or radiology.

SCOPE was created with a simple objective — to bring together expertise, experience and innovation and make advanced endoscopy more accessible to the practising endoscopist.

SCOPE 2027 continues this journey. Through live demonstrations, interactive discussions, hands-on training, challenging cases and interaction with experts from across the world, we hope to create an environment where knowledge is not merely presented but experienced and applied.

I invite you to join us in Chennai for three days of learning, interaction and innovation. Let us continue to push the boundaries of what endoscopy can achieve.

Warm regards,
Dr. C. Palanivelu
Chairman, GEM Hospitals, India
